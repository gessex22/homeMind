# HomeMind — Delivery note

November 4, 2026 · [Repository](https://github.com/gessex22/homeMind)

## Current demo

A browser prototype where a simulated robot explores a scanned room, builds a map and moves toward selected destinations. On request, Muse Spark and SAM analyze its camera view to identify a visible object and enable an approach when enough information is available.

The AI calls are real. The camera view, distance readings and robot movement come from the simulation. The demo still requires user supervision and may stop with parts of the room unexplored.

## Intended outcome

A physical home robot that can understand “go to the charger,” find it and return to it. Searching beyond the current view, remembering objects reliably, real-world navigation and docking remain future work.
